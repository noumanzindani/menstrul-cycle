So here's what I built
Ask deeply.
Predict honestly.
Explain personally.
  • No fertility percentage, ever. False precision reads as permission.
  • Predictions vanish below confidence rather than degrading quietly.
  • A disclaimer on every fertility surface — the thing the research found nobody does.
  • An 11-question intake so the app can say something true on day one.
  • Encrypted on-device, verified on real hardware, not just in tests.
