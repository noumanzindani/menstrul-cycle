Cycle phases (28 days)

Menstrual

Days 1–5

Hormone levels are at their lowest. You may feel more tired and inward-facing.

Follicular

Days 6–13

Estrogen rises, often bringing higher energy and a more outgoing mood.

Ovulatory

Days 14–16

The body prepares for potential conception. Energy and confidence often peak.

Luteal

Days 17–28

Progesterone rises. You might notice changes in mood, skin, or energy levels.
