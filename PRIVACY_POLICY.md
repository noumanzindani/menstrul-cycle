# LunaTrack Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 16 July 2026_

LunaTrack ("the app", "we") is a menstrual and cycle tracking app. This policy
explains what data the app handles and how. **Short version: your health data
stays on your device. We do not have a server and we cannot see your data.**

## What data the app stores

All information you enter — periods, flow, symptoms, moods, **sexual activity**,
notes, reminders, and settings — is stored **only on your device**, in the app's
private storage.

- Your data is **encrypted at rest**. The app's database is encrypted on your
  device with a 256-bit key that is generated on your device, stored in the
  operating system's keystore, and never leaves it. We never see this key, and
  neither does anyone else — it is not backed up, transmitted, or recoverable by
  us. If you lose the device, the data is not readable without it.
- We do **not** operate a server or cloud database.
- Your cycle and health data is **never uploaded, synced, or shared** by us.
- There is **no account and no sign-in**. We do not collect your name, email,
  or phone number.
- Some of what you can log — such as **sexual activity** and health symptoms — is
  especially sensitive. It is treated exactly the same way as everything else:
  kept on your device only, never transmitted, and excluded by default from the
  doctor-summary PDF.

## Data you choose to export

If you use the "Export summary for your doctor" feature, the app generates a PDF
**on your device** and hands it to the system share sheet. Where that file goes
next (email, a messaging app, saving to files) is controlled entirely by you.

## Advertising

The free version of LunaTrack shows ads via **Google AdMob**.

- We request **non-personalized ads only**. We do **not** send your health data,
  cycle data, or any personal identifiers to the ad network.
- On first launch we present a consent choice (Google's User Messaging Platform)
  where required by law (e.g. GDPR regions).
- Ads are shown only on the Home, Calendar, and Settings screens. We do **not**
  show ads on the symptom-logging or health-insights screens.
- Purchasing **Premium** removes all ads.

AdMob may process limited technical data (such as a device advertising
identifier and coarse, non-personalized signals) to serve and measure ads. See
Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

## Purchases

Premium is a one-time in-app purchase processed by the Google Play Store. We do
not receive or store your payment details; the store handles the transaction.

## Permissions

- **Notifications** — to deliver the reminders you enable. Optional.
- **Biometric/device credential** — only if you enable the in-app lock, to
  unlock the app. Handled by your device; we never receive biometric data.

## Deleting your data

Because everything is local, you are always in control:

- **Settings → Delete all my data** permanently erases everything in the app.
- Uninstalling the app also removes all stored data from your device.

## Children

LunaTrack is intended for people who menstruate, which can include teenagers. We
only ever request **non-personalized** ads, and we collect no personal
information, so the app does not knowingly build a profile of any user.

## Medical disclaimer

LunaTrack provides estimates and general wellness information. It is **not a
contraceptive method**, and it does **not** provide medical diagnosis or advice.
Always consult a qualified clinician for medical concerns.
